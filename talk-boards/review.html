Topic starters: teacher review

These boards were drafted with AI, and the pictures were chosen by hand from the Mulberry and OpenMoji sets. None of them reaches children as "checked" until a teacher approves it here.

For each board, look at the pictures, tap ▶ to hear a word, and choose Approve or Needs changes. Say what to change in the comment box: a better word, a picture that doesn't read well, a missing word. Greyed squares are the core words, which stay in the same places on every board.

Your choices are saved in this browser as you go. When you're done, type your name at the top, tap Export decisions, and send the file it saves. Each export only carries the decisions you haven't exported yet.
