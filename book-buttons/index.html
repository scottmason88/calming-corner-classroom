Book Buttons

Button sets

One set per page of the book, or one for the whole book. Children step through them with the big arrows; a set with more than 12 buttons shows 12 at a time.

      How it works

      1. Open the book or activity in its own tab or app (Google Slides, PowerPoint, a PDF). It stays exactly as it is.
      2. Grab pictures from it: pick its tab or window, then drag a box around a picture.
      3. Make buttons with those pictures, a word, what it says and a colour, set by set.
      4. Download panel, open the file, and tap Float. The buttons stay on top of the activity in their own small window.

      Pictures you grabbed

      Pictures cut from a frozen picture of the activity. Nothing is ever changed in the activity itself.

      Drag a box around a picture

      Each box becomes a picture you can use for a button.
